MGOPS is an open-source private server implementation for the original Metal Gear Online (MGO1), included with Metal Gear Solid 3: Subsistence on the PlayStation 2. The project powers community revival infrastructure such as SaveMGO, allowing players on original PS2 hardware and emulators to connect, authenticate, host lobbies, and play online multiplayer matches.
